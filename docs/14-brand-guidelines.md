# 14 — Brand Guidelines

|                  |                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Name**         | **Nura Skin**. "Nura" evokes _light_ (from the Arabic/Aramaic root _nur_, "light") and reads softly in English. It suggests the healthy glow of well-cared-for skin, not a filter. |
| **Brand idea**   | _Clarity for your skin._ Clear skin, and clear answers.                                                                                                                            |
| **Tagline**      | **"Your routine, explained."**                                                                                                                                                     |

## 2. Brand personality

Nura is **the friend who happens to be a cosmetic chemist**: knowledgeable but never condescending, honest even when it costs a sale, calm and warm.

| We are                                                  | We are not                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Knowledgeable**: grounded in ingredients and evidence | Clinical, cold, jargon-heavy                    |
| **Honest**: we say when you _don't_ need something      | Hype-driven, miracle-promising                  |
| **Calm**: unhurried, reassuring                         | Urgent, FOMO, countdown timers                  |
| **Warm**: human, encouraging                            | Saccharine, overly cute                         |
| **Minimal**: edited, intentional                        | Sparse to the point of cold, or luxury-snobbish |
| **Inclusive**: every skin type, tone, gender and age    | "Anti-aging" shaming, gendered defaults         |

**Archetype:** _The Sage_ (primary) with _The Caregiver_ (secondary).

## 3. Tone of voice

### 3.1 Principles

1. **Explain, don't impress.** Every claim comes with a reason. "Niacinamide helps regulate oil" beats "Revolutionary oil control."
2. **Say the honest thing.** "You probably don't need a toner." "This will tingle for the first week."
3. **Talk like a person.** Use contractions and second person ("you"). Short sentences.
4. **Be specific.** Numbers, ingredients and timeframes, all accurate.
5. **Reassure, never scare.** Frame skin concerns as normal and manageable.

### 3.2 Vocabulary

| Use                                                                       | Avoid                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "helps", "supports", "visibly", "the look of" (compliant cosmetic claims) | "cures", "treats", "heals", "clinically proven" (unless substantiated), "medical-grade" |
| "breakouts", "blemishes", "post-acne marks"                               | "bad skin", "problem skin", "flaws"                                                     |
| "fine lines", "texture", "firmness"                                       | "anti-aging", "fight wrinkles", "youthful again"                                        |
| "fragrance-free", "formulated without X"                                  | "clean", "non-toxic", "chemical-free" (unscientific fearmongering)                      |
| "routine", "steps"                                                        | "regimen" (clinical), "hacks"                                                           |
| "Skin type: combination"                                                  | "Normal skin" as a moral ideal                                                          |

### 3.3 Tone by context

| Context             | Tone                 | Example                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home hero           | Confident, calm      | "Your skin, understood. Get a routine built for you in three minutes, with every step explained."                                                              |
| Finder questions    | Friendly, curious    | "How does your skin feel by midday? There's no wrong answer."                                                                                                  |
| Finder results      | Expert, personal     | "Because your breakouts cluster along your jaw and you mentioned stinging from acids, we chose azelaic acid, which clears pores gently while calming redness." |
| PDP                 | Informative, precise | "15% ethyl ascorbic acid, a stable form of vitamin C, brightens the look of dull skin and fades dark spots over 8–12 weeks."                                   |
| Safety              | Caring, direct       | "Because you're pregnant, we've left out retinoids. Here's what we chose instead."                                                                             |
| Errors              | Accountable, helpful | "That didn't go through. Your card wasn't charged. Try again, or use a different payment method."                                                              |
| Empty states        | Light, encouraging   | "Your wishlist is empty, which your bathroom shelf will appreciate. Find your routine?"                                                                        |
| Subscription cancel | Respectful, no guilt | "Sorry to see you go. Want to skip a delivery instead? Either way is fine."                                                                                    |
| Admin UI            | Neutral, precise     | "Refund $24.00 to Visa •••• 4242? This can't be undone."                                                                                                       |

### 3.4 Grammar & mechanics

Sentence case for all headings and buttons ("Add to cart", not "Add To Cart"). Oxford comma. Numerals for all numbers in UI. Concentrations as "10%". "SPF 50" (no hyphen). Product names in title case without "The". Ingredient INCI names in their standard casing.

## 4. Logo direction

**Primary logo: wordmark.** "nura" in lowercase set in a custom-drawn soft serif derived from Fraunces (SOFT 100, weight 400), with generous tracking (+2%). A **dew drop**, a small teardrop-shaped mark, sits above-right of the final "a" and doubles as a highlight (light reflecting on skin).

**Secondary lockup:** "nura" + "SKIN" in Inter 600, tracked +20%, set small beneath it (for packaging and the footer).

**Symbol (brand mark):** the dew drop alone inside a circle, used for the favicon, app icon, social avatars and bottle caps.

| Spec         | Value                                                                                                                       |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Clear space  | Height of the "n" x-height on all sides                                                                                     |
| Minimum size | Wordmark 72 px / 18 mm wide; symbol 16 px / 5 mm                                                                            |
| Colours      | Ink on porcelain (primary); porcelain on ink (reversed); clay-600 on porcelain (special use: packaging foil)                |
| Don't        | Stretch, outline, add gradients or shadows, place on busy photography without a scrim, rotate, recolour outside the palette |

**Asset deliverables:** `logo-wordmark.svg`, `logo-wordmark-reversed.svg`, `logo-lockup.svg`, `logo-symbol.svg`, favicons (see 17 §9). The logo SVGs are designed by hand (vector); AI image generation is **not** used for the logo, because AI output isn't reliable for crisp, ownable vector marks. Asset inventory items `LOGO-*` specify this.

## 5. Packaging style

| Element                 | Direction                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Materials**           | Frosted recycled glass (serums, 30 ml dropper bottles); soft-touch recycled aluminium tubes (cleansers, SPF); frosted glass jars with aluminium lids (creams); refill pouches (matte paper-laminate, recyclable)                                                                                                      |
| **Colour system**       | Base container **frosted porcelain-white** or clear frosted glass. **Cap / band colour codes the routine slot:** Cleanse = sand `#E4D9CA`, Treat = clay `#B8785A`, Moisturize = sage `#7D8C6E`, Protect = dew `#4E6E6A`. This way customers learn their routine order by colour.                                      |
| **Label layout**        | Top: "nura" wordmark. Middle: product name in Fraunces (e.g. _Glow Serum_). Below: the **key active and %** in Inter 600 ("15% Vitamin C"). A step indicator circle with a number (1 Cleanse / 2 Treat / 3 Moisturize / 4 Protect) and an AM/PM sun/moon icon. Bottom: size. Back: full INCI, how to use, PAO symbol. |
| **Typography on pack**  | Ink on white; minimal. No claims clutter, maximum 3 lines on the front.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Secondary packaging** | Unbleached kraft mailer box with an ink-printed dew-drop pattern inside the lid, and a printed "Your routine" card listing the customer's steps (personalized from `RoutineStep`, P2 via fulfilment integration).                                                                                                     |
| **Sustainability cues** | Refills for moisturizers; recyclability icon; no plastic windows                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

These specs drive the product image prompts in `17-asset-inventory.md`, so all packshots look like one coherent line.

## 6. Photography style

| Attribute         | Direction                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Light**         | Soft, directional natural light (large window, morning); gentle shadows; no harsh flash; warm colour temperature (~5000K)                                                                                                                                      |
| **Palette**       | Porcelain, linen, sand, stone, clay, sage. Backgrounds are plaster walls, travertine, limestone, linen fabric, water.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Composition**   | Generous negative space (room for copy); off-center subjects; low-angle "plinth" shots for products; overhead flat-lays for routines                                                                                                                           |
| **Props**         | Natural materials only: travertine blocks, water droplets, oat, olive branches, eucalyptus sprigs, a folded linen towel, a ceramic dish. **No** glitter, flowers explosions, fake fruit splash or synthetic props.                                             |
| **Texture shots** | Macro swatches on glass or stone that show real formula texture (gel, cream, fluid, balm)                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **People**        | Real, unretouched-looking skin with visible texture, pores and freckles; diverse ages (20s–60s), skin tones (Fitzpatrick I–VI), genders; natural expressions; the hands-and-face routine moments. **Never** before/after composites or "perfect" plastic skin. |
| **Retouching**    | Minimal: remove temporary blemishes only if they distract; never smooth skin texture; never alter shape                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Mood words**    | _Calm. Clear. Honest. Sunlit. Tactile._                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

**AI-generated imagery policy (for this fictional brand):** all photography in the portfolio build is AI-generated with the prompts in the asset inventory, then art-directed for consistency (same lighting, props, palette). Generated people imagery must look natural. It is only used for lifestyle and marketing contexts, **never** to depict product results (no fake before/afters), and every image is reviewed for artefacts (hands, text on packaging, which must be clean or added later in post).

## 7. Illustration style

| Attribute            | Direction                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Technique**        | Single-weight line drawings (1.5 px at 24 px scale), round caps, occasional soft filled shapes in tints (clay-100, sage-100, dew-100) offset from the line (a "misregistered print" feel)                     |
| **Subjects**         | Ingredient botanicals (oat, centella, sunflower), molecules simplified to friendly geometric forms (hyaluronic chains, vitamin C ring), skin-layer cross-sections for the science page, empty-state vignettes |
| **Style references** | Botanical line illustration meets scientific diagram; minimal, airy                                                                                                                                           |
| **Colour**           | Stroke ink (light) / porcelain (dark) via `currentColor`; fills from tints only                                                                                                                               |
| **Don't**            | Gradients, 3D renders, cartoon faces, mascots                                                                                                                                                                 |

Illustrations are delivered as SVG. AI generation may be used for **concept sketches** only, then redrawn as clean vectors (listed in the asset inventory as "AI concept → vector redraw").

## 8. Marketing style

| Element        | Direction                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Headlines**  | Fraunces, short, often a quiet contrast: "Fewer steps. Better skin." / "Retinal, but gentle."                                                                         |
| **Layout**     | Big image, big whitespace, one message per asset, one CTA                                                                                                             |
| **Proof**      | Ingredient % + mechanism > influencer quotes; verified-review snippets with skin type ("Combination skin · 4 weeks")                                                  |
| **Promotions** | Tasteful: "15% off your first routine" in ink on linen. No red starbursts, no countdown timers, no fake scarcity. Sales are rare (2–3 per year) to protect the brand. |
| **Email**      | 600 px, porcelain background, one hero image, headline, 2–3 short paragraphs, one ink button; plain-text-friendly                                                     |
| **Compliance** | Cosmetic claims only; substantiate "% of users saw…" claims or don't make them; FTC disclosures on creator content                                                    |

## 9. Social media style

| Platform      | Content pillars                                                                                                                                                                                           | Format                                                                                 |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Instagram** | (1) Ingredient explainers (carousel: "What 10% niacinamide actually does"), (2) Routine flat-lays by concern, (3) Texture macros, (4) Real reviews as quote cards, (5) "Don't buy this if…" honesty posts | 4:5 feed posts, 9:16 stories/reels; grid alternates photo · typographic card · texture |
| **TikTok**    | Finder walkthroughs ("I let an AI build my routine — here's what it left out"), myth-busting with a cosmetic chemist voice, application ASMR                                                              | 9:16, native captions, first 1 s hook                                                  |
| **Pinterest** | Routine infographics by concern → SEO landing pages                                                                                                                                                       | 2:3 pins                                                                               |

**Visual templates** (see 17 §7): quote card, ingredient explainer carousel (cover + 4 slides), routine flat-lay with step labels, and a story template with a finder CTA sticker.
**Voice on social:** same as the brand, slightly more playful; emojis sparingly (max 1, from a limited set: 💧 ☀️ 🌙 ✓).
**Community rules:** answer skincare questions with general guidance, and refer medical questions to a dermatologist (template replies).
